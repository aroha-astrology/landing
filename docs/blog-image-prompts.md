# Blog image prompts

58 articles are published. 56 have a real image (36 illustrations, 19
photographs, 1 app screenshot). Two astrology articles still show
code-generated art from `scripts/blog-art/`: `sade-sati-saturn-transit` and
`vedic-astrology-yoga-planetary-combinations`. Their prompts are at the end
of this file. The other 13 prompts below were used on 2026-10-04 and are
kept for regenerating an image.

Last updated 2026-10-04.

## How to use

1. Set the image shape to **landscape 16:9** in the tool itself before
   generating. An earlier batch came out portrait (9:16) and had to be
   centred on a blurred backdrop to fit the hero frame.
2. Paste the **style block** for the group once, then one article prompt at
   a time.
3. Reject any image with: writing of any kind, logos, a recognisable real
   person, a deity's face in close-up, or a real brand's product.
4. Save each as `<slug>.png` or `<slug>.jpg`, exactly as the filename below,
   and send the folder. They're converted to 1600×900 WebP, the Gemini
   sparkle is removed, and `hero:` is set in the article. The code-generated
   art stays as the fallback.

The images are recorded in `public/assets/LICENSES.md` as "AI-generated for
Aroha" when added.

## Vastu and Puja photographs (12)

### Style block (paste first)

> I'm making a series of blog header photos for an Indian Vastu and Puja
> site. Keep one style across all of them: bright, modern editorial interior
> photography, like a current home-design magazine. Contemporary Indian city
> flats from the 2020s: clean lines, light oak or teak wood, off-white
> walls, natural stone, linen, a few brass and terracotta accents, green
> plants. Soft natural daylight, true-to-life colours, sharp focus on the
> subject, gentle depth of field, an uncluttered composition with some empty
> space on one side. Photorealistic, high detail. No text, letters, numbers,
> signs, logos or watermarks anywhere. No identifiable faces: hands or
> out-of-focus figures only. No close-ups of deity faces. Not a palace,
> temple or heritage haveli. No heavy HDR, no orange glow filter, no fantasy
> lighting. Landscape 16:9.
