Your birth chart is fixed the moment you're born — but the planets keep moving. **Gochar**, or transit, is how Vedic astrology tracks that ongoing movement and reads it against the chart you were born with.

## What is Gochar?

Two things are always in play together in transit analysis:

**Natal chart** — the planetary positions at the exact moment of your birth, permanently fixed.

**Current planetary transits** — the planets' actual positions in the sky right now, constantly moving.

Gochar is the practice of overlaying today's planetary positions onto your natal chart — checking which of your natal houses each moving planet is currently passing through, and what that's traditionally understood to activate.

## The major transits worth tracking

- **Saturn** — slow-moving, associated with long structural periods and lessons
- **Jupiter** — slow-moving, associated with growth and expansion cycles
- **Rahu** and **Ketu** — the lunar nodes, moving in reverse through the zodiac
- **Mars** — faster-moving, tied to bursts of energy and action
- **Venus**, **Mercury** — faster still, shorter-lived influences
- **Sun**, **Moon** — the fastest, shifting focus day to day and month to month

## Why slow-moving planets are especially important

Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu can spend a year or more in a single sign, which is exactly why they're given particular weight when studying longer-term transit themes — a fast planet's transit passes in days, but a slow planet's transit through a sensitive house can shape an entire year or more.

## Transit + Dasha, together

Transits are rarely read in isolation. Most Vedic astrology approaches consider Dashas and transits together rather than using a transit by itself for detailed timing — the Dasha sets which planet's themes are already active in your life, and the transit shows what's currently triggering them.
