Walk into most gemstone shops and you'll get a recommendation based on your Western birth month. Vedic astrology works differently: a gemstone is traditionally prescribed to strengthen a specific planet's influence in your own birth chart — not your sign, and not a universal birthstone chart.

## The Navaratna: nine gems for nine planets

Classical Jyotish assigns one primary gemstone to each of the nine grahas:

- **Ruby** — Sun
- **Pearl** — Moon
- **Red Coral** — Mars
- **Emerald** — Mercury
- **Yellow Sapphire** — Jupiter
- **Diamond (or White Sapphire)** — Venus
- **Blue Sapphire** — Saturn
- **Hessonite (Gomed)** — Rahu
- **Cat's Eye (Lehsunia)** — Ketu

Together these nine are called the Navaratna.

## Why it's not "wear your sign's stone"

A gemstone is traditionally recommended to boost a planet that is a genuine, well-placed benefic for that specific chart — one that's capable of doing good but needs support, not simply the planet ruling your Sun or Moon sign. Recommending a gem for a planet that's poorly placed or functionally unfavorable for a given chart is generally considered counterproductive rather than neutral, which is the main reason generic "your sign, your stone" advice doesn't map onto real Vedic practice.

## Blue Sapphire's reputation

Among the nine, Blue Sapphire (Neelam) for Saturn has the strongest reputation for unpredictable results — traditionally described as fast-acting in both directions. Because of this, it's commonly recommended to trial-wear it briefly before committing to it long-term, more so than any of the other eight stones.

## Authenticity is the part most guides skip

The traditional efficacy claims around gemstones assume a natural stone — untreated or minimally treated, of adequate carat weight, worn in an appropriate metal, touching the skin. A synthetic, heavily treated, or undersized stone doesn't carry the same weight in the tradition it comes from, which is why verifying what you're actually buying matters as much as picking the right planet in the first place.
