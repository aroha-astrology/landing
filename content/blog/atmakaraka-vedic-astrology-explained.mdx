Most of what's covered in a typical Kundli reading — houses, signs, dignity, Dasha — comes from the Parashari system. The **Atmakaraka** comes from a different branch entirely: Jaimini astrology, which reads the chart through significators rather than fixed house rulerships, and treats this one planet as the key to the chart's deeper themes.

## What Is Atmakaraka?

Atmakaraka literally means "significator of the soul" — in Jaimini astrology, it's the planet believed to carry the native's core life themes and deepest motivations, distinct from what any single house or sign placement shows on its own.

## The Meaning of Atmakaraka in Jaimini Astrology

Where Parashari astrology reads a fixed house (like the 1st house) for self and identity, Jaimini astrology instead identifies significator planets by degree — planets that earn their role through their specific position in this one chart, rather than through a fixed rule that applies the same way to everyone. The Atmakaraka is the most important of these Jaimini significators, and forms the starting point for several Jaimini-specific techniques, including the Karakamsha chart used to find an Ishta Devata.

## How to Find Your Atmakaraka

Look at the degree each of the seven classical planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) occupies within its own sign — every planet sits somewhere between 0° and 30° in whatever sign it's in. Whichever planet holds the **highest degree** among them is the Atmakaraka.

## Does Rahu Count? Why Different Methods Give Different Results

This is a genuine methodological split, not a settled question. Some Jaimini practitioners include Rahu in the degree comparison (Ketu is conventionally excluded even in these methods); others restrict the comparison to the seven classical planets only. Because Rahu often sits at a high degree in many charts, including or excluding it can change which planet comes out as Atmakaraka — which is also why two different tools or astrologers can occasionally disagree on the result for the same birth data.

## Meaning of Each Planet as Atmakaraka

### Sun Atmakaraka

Traditionally linked to themes of authority, identity, ego and self-direction as the chart's central life lesson.

### Moon Atmakaraka

Traditionally linked to emotional depth, the mind, and themes of nurturing or being nurtured as central to the native's path.

### Mars Atmakaraka

Traditionally linked to courage, drive and assertiveness — a life theme built around action and independence.

### Mercury Atmakaraka

Traditionally linked to intellect, communication and adaptability as the core thread running through the native's growth.

### Jupiter Atmakaraka

Traditionally linked to wisdom, teaching and higher belief systems — often read as a life oriented toward knowledge or guidance of others.

### Venus Atmakaraka

Traditionally linked to relationships, harmony, and aesthetic or creative sensibility as central life themes.

### Saturn Atmakaraka

Traditionally linked to discipline, endurance and lessons learned through effort and restriction over time.

### Rahu Atmakaraka

Where included, traditionally linked to unconventional ambition and themes the native pursues outside inherited patterns — read cautiously given the disagreement over whether Rahu should be counted at all.

## Atmakaraka and Karakamsha

The **Karakamsha** is a specific point derived from the Atmakaraka — the sign the Atmakaraka occupies in the Navamsa (D9) chart, treated as a second, Jaimini-specific ascendant for reading deeper soul-level themes. See [divisional charts and the Navamsa](/blog/divisional-charts-navamsa-guide) for how the D9 itself is calculated.

## Atmakaraka and Ishta Devata

One of the main traditional uses of the Atmakaraka and Karakamsha together is identifying an **Ishta Devata** — a personal deity indicated by the chart.
