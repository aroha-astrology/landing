If the Lagna is the starting point of your birth chart, the **12 houses (bhavas)** are the map it's drawn on. Every planet in your chart sits in exactly one house, and which house it's in changes what that planet is actually talking about in your life.

## The 12 houses, one by one

- **1st — Tanu Bhava:** self, body, personality
- **2nd — Dhana Bhava:** wealth, family, speech
- **3rd — Sahaja Bhava:** courage, siblings, communication
- **4th — Sukha Bhava:** home, mother, property
- **5th — Putra Bhava:** intelligence, creativity, children
- **6th — Ripu/Roga Bhava:** competition, debts, disease
- **7th — Kalatra Bhava:** marriage, partnerships
- **8th — Ayu Bhava:** transformation, longevity, hidden matters
- **9th — Dharma/Bhagya Bhava:** fortune, dharma, higher learning
- **10th — Karma Bhava:** career, profession, status
- **11th — Labha Bhava:** gains, income, networks
- **12th — Vyaya Bhava:** expenditure, isolation, foreign lands

A planet's placement in one of these houses tells you *where in your life* its themes tend to show up. Jupiter in the 10th house, for instance, is read very differently from Jupiter in the 12th — same planet, different arena.

## Kendra houses

The 1st, 4th, 7th and 10th houses are the **Kendras**, or angular houses. They form the structural backbone of the chart, and planets placed here — or ruling here — are generally considered to gain strength and prominence.

## Trikona houses

The 1st, 5th and 9th houses are the **Trikonas**, or trine houses, traditionally associated with fortune, merit and auspiciousness. The 1st house belongs to both groups, which is part of why the Lagna carries so much weight in a chart reading.

## Dusthana houses

The 6th, 8th and 12th houses are the **Dusthanas** — houses tied to struggle, loss, transformation and endings. They aren't simply "bad" houses; they're where a chart's harder lessons and turning points tend to concentrate, and they're read carefully rather than dismissed.

## Upachaya houses

The 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th houses are the **Upachayas**, or "growth" houses — areas of life that traditionally tend to improve with effort and time, even when a planet placed there starts out weak.

## Why the same planet behaves differently in different houses

This is the core reason houses matter as much as signs and planets: a planet's basic nature doesn't change from chart to chart, but its house placement determines which part of your life it's actively influencing. Venus in the 7th speaks to partnership in a very direct way; Venus in the 2nd speaks more to wealth, family and speech. Understanding the house system is what turns "I have strong Jupiter" into an actual, specific reading of your life.
