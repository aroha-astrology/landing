A Vedic birth chart holds a lot of information at once, which is exactly why it can feel overwhelming the first time you look at one. Reading it isn't about grasping everything simultaneously — it's about working through it in a consistent order. Here's that order, step by step.

## Step 1 — Check your birth details

Every downstream calculation depends on three inputs: your date, your exact time, and your location of birth. Accuracy matters most for time — even a small error can shift your Ascendant and, with it, every house in your chart.

## Step 2 — Identify the Lagna

Your Ascendant is the anchor point for the entire chart — the sign rising on the horizon at your birth moment. See [What Is Lagna?](/blog/what-is-lagna-ascendant) for the full explanation.

## Step 3 — Understand the 12 houses

Once you know your Lagna, you know which house is which. Read through [12 Houses in Vedic Astrology](/blog/12-houses-vedic-astrology) to see what each one governs.

## Step 4 — Locate the planets

Find where each of the nine Grahas — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu — sits by sign and house. This is the raw data your chart is built from.

## Step 5 — Assess planetary strength

For each planet, look at:

- Exaltation or debilitation
- Whether it's in its own sign
- Combustion
- Retrograde status
- Conjunctions with other planets
- Aspects it's receiving

This step tells you how strongly each planet is likely to express its themes.

## Step 6 — Examine important yogas

Check for the major planetary combinations — Raj Yoga, Dhan Yoga, Manglik Dosha and others — that shape how the chart's individual pieces interact as a whole.

## Step 7 — Check the Navamsa

The D9 chart adds a finer layer of detail, especially for marriage and inner strength. See [Divisional Charts: Navamsa (D9) and the Vargas Explained](/blog/divisional-charts-navamsa-guide).

## Step 8 — Check your Vimshottari Dasha

Dashas tell you *when* different planetary themes are active across your life. See the full guide: [How to Read Your Vimshottari Dasha](/blog/vimshottari-dasha-guide).

## Step 9 — Check current transits

Overlay today's planetary positions onto your natal chart to see what's currently being activated. See [Planetary Transits Explained: What Is Gochar?](/blog/planetary-transits-gochar-vedic-astrology)

## Step 10 — Interpret the chart as a whole

This is the step that's easiest to skip and most important not to: a single placement should never be used to make an absolute prediction on its own. A complete interpretation weighs the interaction between your Lagna, houses, house lords, planets, planetary strength, aspects, yogas, Dashas, transits and divisional charts — together, not one at a time.
