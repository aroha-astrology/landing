Once you're comfortable reading planets, houses and signs individually, Vedic astrology introduces a layer that ties them together: **Yoga**. Not to be confused with the physical practice, a Yoga in Jyotish generally refers to a specific relationship or combination involving planets, houses, signs and their lords — one that classical texts associate with a particular kind of outcome.

## An important distinction

Not every yoga automatically guarantees a specific result. A chart can technically contain a favorable yoga and still need the rest of the picture — the overall chart, each planet's strength, its aspects, and the timing brought by Dashas and transits — before that combination actually plays out the way the texts describe. Yogas describe *potential*, not a fixed outcome independent of everything else in the chart.

## The major yogas worth knowing

- **[Raja Yoga](/blog/raj-yoga-vedic-astrology)** — combinations associated with authority, achievement and favorable status, typically formed through connections between Kendra and Trikona house lords
- **[Dhana Yoga](/blog/dhan-yoga-wealth-vedic-astrology)** — combinations associated with wealth, usually involving the 2nd, 11th, 5th and 9th houses and their lords
- **Gajakesari Yoga** — formed by a specific relationship between the Moon and Jupiter, traditionally linked to intelligence and reputation
- **Pancha Mahapurusha Yoga** — a group of five yogas formed when Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn is strongly placed in a Kendra in its own or exaltation sign
- **Budhaditya Yoga** — formed by a conjunction of the Sun and Mercury, associated with intellect and communication skill
- **Viparita Raja Yoga** — a less intuitive yoga where lords of the difficult 6th, 8th or 12th houses combine in a way traditionally read as turning adversity into an advantage
- **Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga** — a combination that cancels or modifies a planet's debilitation under specific conditions, discussed in more depth alongside [exalted and debilitated planets](/blog/exalted-debilitated-planets)
